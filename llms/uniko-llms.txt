# Uniko

> Uniko is a Mexican gift registry (mesa de regalos) SaaS for weddings, events, and baby showers. Couples receive gifts as cash deposited to their bank account, plus a personalized wedding website, save-the-date invitations, and WhatsApp-based guest confirmation. Uniko is a 500 Global portfolio company. It is a consumer-facing platform and publishes no public API, developer documentation, or machine-readable specs.
