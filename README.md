# Uniko

Uniko is a Mexican gift registry (mesa de regalos) SaaS platform for weddings, events, and baby showers, letting couples convert gifts into cash deposited directly to their bank account. The platform bundles a personalized wedding website, save-the-date digital invitations, WhatsApp-based guest confirmation, and planning tools, and also offers store-credit gift registries for ecommerce shops. Uniko is consumer-facing and publishes no public API or developer documentation.
